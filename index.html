Recognizable Symptoms

Signs to Request a Foundation Inspection

Uneven floors, sticking doors or windows, wall cracks, and trim separation are reasons to request an on-site evaluation — not an online diagnosis. Symptoms may have different causes that a property evaluation determines.

1

Doors & Windows That Stick

When piers settle, the frame twists out of alignment. Interior doors drag, refuse to latch, or swing open on their own.

2

Uneven or Bouncy Floors

Walking feels like an incline or trampoline. Dropped pier blocks or compromised shims create low spots and floor deflection.

3

Marriage Line Gaps

In double-wides, the center joining seam pulls apart due to differential settling, opening ceiling cracks and floor transitions.

4

Wall & Drywall Cracks

Stress fractures branching diagonally from door corners, window frames, or ceiling joints as the structure flexes.

5

Separation Around Trim

Crown molding, baseboards, or exterior fascia pulling away from walls — signaling active foundation movement.

6

Skirting Buckling or Gaps

Skirting panels bowing under weight or hanging inches above the ground line as East Texas clay soil shifts.
